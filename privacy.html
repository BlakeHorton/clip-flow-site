Privacy policy

clip-flow is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own social media accounts.

  • It uses Google's YouTube Data API to upload videos and read basic channel information (the channel name) for the signed-in account.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. They are never sold, shared or sent to anyone other than the platform they belong to.
  • No analytics, advertising or third-party tracking is used.
  • Access can be removed at any time from Google account permissions.

clip-flow's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
