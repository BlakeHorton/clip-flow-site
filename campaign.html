Campaign Flow

Campaign Flow is a separate personal tool from clip-flow. It publishes licensed clips made for paid clipping campaigns (where the rights holder supplies the footage and pays per view) to its owner's own campaign YouTube channels. It uses YouTube API Services.

It is used only by its owner, uploads only to the owner's campaign channels, and never to the channels used by clip-flow. It has its own Google Cloud project.

By using Campaign Flow you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Campaign Flow privacy policy and the Google Privacy Policy.
